# Credits

FLYGLINJE builds on the work of people who reverse-engineered the IKEA KORTLINJE before us. Thank you!

## VIPnytt/Kortlinje

<https://github.com/VIPnytt/Kortlinje> – MIT License

Connector pinouts (TOP-KEY, POWER, BUZ), the USB cable colours and further board signals (light sensor, climate sensor, wake line) come from this project's wiki.

## kobebeefg/ikea-clock-lab

<https://github.com/kobebeefg/ikea-clock-lab> – no license published

The display-driver pads and the cut-and-desolder method for buttons and buzzer were first documented here. Because the repository has no license, FLYGLINJE uses it **as a reference only**: no code, text or images were copied. All wiring graphics and descriptions in this repository are our own.
